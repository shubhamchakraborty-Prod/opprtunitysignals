# Noida Sector 62 / 63 Industry Opportunity Explorer: demonstration note

**ConveGenius.AI · internal research pilot · prepared October 2026 · not an official employment statistic**

## Purpose

Help ITI and skilling planners see which occupations employers in Noida Sectors 62 and 63 are publicly advertising, what they ask for, and which openings an employer has actually confirmed, before any course or cohort decision.

## What the pilot shows today

| Item | Status on 16 Oct 2026 |
|---|---|
| Public hiring signals | 17 current records (Sector 62: 8, Sector 63: 9) plus 1 older post, all with source links |
| Employer-confirmed openings | **0**. No employer has been contacted yet, so no vacancy total is shown |
| Sector 62 pattern (sample) | Contact centre, office administration, IT operations and sales roles |
| Sector 63 pattern (sample) | Electronics and mechanical production, quality inspection, sales and digital roles |
| Trends, worker sentiment, coverage of ITI pass-outs | Not available. The app explains which data is missing |

## How to read it

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Source fact** | What the advert or employer said, with date and link |
| **Inference** | Our interpretation (occupation, relevant ITI trade, location reliability) |
| **Recommendation** | The next step: plan, call the employer, or keep as context only |

A job advert is a signal, not a vacancy. An opening counts only after the employer confirms it and a second reviewer approves the record.

## Demonstration path (10 minutes)

1. **Quick start:** choose Sector 63 and "ITI Electronics Mechanic"; the list narrows to ads that may suit that learner.
2. **Jobs map:** click a dot or ad; read what the ad says, what we think and what to do next; open the original ad.
3. **Compare 62 and 63:** type of work, skills asked for, and why no trend is shown yet.
4. **Match a learner:** see which ads are worth a call, which need more skills, and which the learner is not eligible for as written.
5. **Next steps:** five ads are flagged "call these employers first"; none is yet ready for planning.
6. **Record an employer call (team sign-in):** the confirmed-jobs count rises only after a second person approves.

## Safeguards

| Safeguard | How |
|---|---|
| No inflated numbers | Search totals and advert claims never become vacancy counts; duplicates counted once |
| Location honesty | Records whose location is only a portal label are marked; adjacent sectors excluded |
| Privacy | No learner personal data; employer contacts restricted; interviews need recorded consent |
| Accountability | Role-based access, second-person approval, append-only audit log |
| Lawful collection | Manual capture and CSV upload; no scraping of gated or private accounts |
