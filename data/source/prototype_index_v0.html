What the evidence means

Do not confuse a job advertisement with a confirmed vacancy.

The public postings below show real occupational signals. To use a vacancy in ITI course or cohort planning, contact the employer to verify the number of openings, terms and expected hiring period.

What stands out from this sample

Sector 62: service, communication, IT operations and office roles appear repeatedly in the sampled listings.

Sector 63: the sample includes electronics, mechanical production, inspection, sales and digital roles.

Sentiment is not measured yet.
Requirements such as night shifts, English proficiency, experience or self-commuting are advertised job terms, not worker-sentiment scores. A genuine sentiment layer needs attributed, consent-appropriate feedback.

Planning interpretation

What could an ITI or workforce planner do with this?

1 · Observe

Identify relevant occupations

Document employer, advertised role, location, qualification, experience and whether the listing is recent.

2 · Verify

Contact the employer

Confirm genuine openings, working conditions and candidate requirements before counting demand.

3 · Plan

Match to training supply

Only after a verified demand baseline, compare the openings with expected ITI pass-outs and training provision.
